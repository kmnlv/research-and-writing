# Research and Writing Portfolio

> Writing samples from Natural Language Processing (Semester 2) and Ethics in Artificial Intelligence (Semester 3), UACJ

## Overview

A single repository for coursework deliverables that are written documents rather than executable notebooks: eleven ethics essays, a critical literature review on large language models, and short handouts that index Colab notebooks for core NLP preprocessing topics. Grouping them here keeps document-only material out of the code repositories while preserving it in the portfolio.

## Repository structure

- `ethics/` - eleven individual essays on ethical and societal aspects of AI.
- `nlp/docs/llm-literature-review.pdf` - critical analysis of five systematic/scoping reviews on LLM applications.
- `nlp/notebooks/` - three short handouts pointing to Colab notebooks on Bag-of-Words / TF-IDF, POS tagging and text preprocessing.

## Ethics essay themes

Foundational principles and values; privacy (including the 23andMe case); justice, diversity and non-discrimination; inclusion, equity, human dignity and autonomy; automated decision-making; and the social, human, labor and economic impact of AI systems.

The literature review covers LLMs in oral education, maxillofacial surgery, multiple-choice question generation, mechanical engineering and neurological decision-making, and discusses the limits of transferring generic LLM findings to narrow domains.

## Notes

All documents are **writing samples in Spanish**; this README is in English. There is no code or dependency to install. Related document-only reports are kept with their corresponding projects instead: see `docs/` in `sbert-embeddings-tsne-clustering`, `mnist-cnn-parameter-analysis`, `imdb-rnn-sentiment-comparison`, `regression-bike-demand-analysis` and `lstm-tep-scrap-forecasting`.
